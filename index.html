Universal HUJI Bilateral Exchange Financial Terms
100% Host Tuition Waived: All students pay standard HUJI tuition only; host institution tuition is completely waived under bilateral agreements. HUJI Grant: Eligible exchange participants typically receive 4,000 NIS financial support.
Official HUJI Exchange Application Guidelines & Steps
Step 1 Check Eligibility Minimum cumulative GPA of 80+. Open to BA students in year 2 or 3 (with at least 10 credits remaining) and MA/PhD students.
Step 2 Choose Up to 3 Destinations Select up to 3 preferred partner universities in order of priority. Check each card's Fact Sheet for language, semester dates, and department rules.
Step 3 Prepare Required Documents English CV, grade transcripts (Hebrew & English), and tailored English motivation letters for each choice (MA/PhD: 1 recommendation letter).
Step 4 Submit via Mobility-Online Submit online through the official portal before the deadline (November 25). Stage 1 is internal HUJI ranking, Stage 2 is host admission.
